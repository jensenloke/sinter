# Sinter brand mark

The mark treats sintering as a visual system: five mineral grains compress into
one compact, durable body. The central grain is the bond point; the surrounding
cells form a stable silhouette that stays recognizable without relying on an
`S`, a flame, or a literal stone. Lime connects it to the existing Cloud UI,
while warm mineral neutrals keep the material reference understated.

## Assets

- `sinter-mark-master.png` — 1254 px transparent raster master
- `sinter-mark-512.png` — large app/marketing icon
- `sinter-mark-192.png` — web app icon
- `sinter-mark-32.png` — favicon-scale asset

The sized files are deterministic downscales of the master. Keep the mark on a
near-black or similarly high-contrast surface and preserve clear space around
its silhouette. These PNGs are the current concept masters; trace and optically
refine the approved geometry before treating it as a final vector trademark.

## Generation prompt

Built-in image generation was used with this final selected prompt:

> Create one original, minimal brand symbol for Sinter. Show sintering as five
> mineral cells compressed and fused into one unbroken, compact geometric body.
> Every cell must share edges with neighboring cells, creating a single
> continuous outer silhouette like a rounded-square monolith; there must be no
> floating pieces or gaps. Use exactly five tightly tessellated rounded polygon
> cells, with a small central lime cell joining four larger cells. Render as an
> ultra-clean, flat, vector-like logo on a genuinely transparent background,
> with a strong favicon-scale silhouette. Use Sinter lime `#C8F46A`, warm
> off-white `#F2F0E8`, muted stone gray `#8A8C84`, and near-black seams
> `#0B0D0C`. No text, letters, obvious S, flames, literal rocks, gradients,
> shadows, 3D effects, mockups, trademarks, or watermarks.

## Verification

All deliverables are square PNG files with RGBA alpha. The 32 px derivative
was retained specifically to verify that the five-cell topology and outer
silhouette survive favicon-scale reduction.
